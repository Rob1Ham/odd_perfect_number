# Perfect-number literature and Lean coverage

Let σ sum the positive divisors, ω count distinct prime divisors, and Ω count them with multiplicity. In the odd-perfect rows, N is an odd natural number satisfying mathlib's `Nat.Perfect`. An exact component is a prime power with the full exponent at that prime. The status beside each result concerns this checkout. The last column identifies the mathematical or computational source.

## Using the corpus

A published inequality can guide a choice of branch. To use it inside a proof, import a checked declaration with the required hypotheses or prove it locally. For a computation-assisted exclusion, record the parent predicate, every child predicate, a coverage theorem for the children, and a certificate checked at every terminal node. A partial factorization can certify a divisor by multiplication or a remainder check. A claim that no other prime can occur requires a further proof.

The corpus is a working bibliography. For larger results without a local declaration, the coverage column records the outstanding proof work. The audit and case ledger describe what the local project can currently use. [Case ledger](LEDGER.md)
