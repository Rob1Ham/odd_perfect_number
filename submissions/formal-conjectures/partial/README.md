# Restricted results for the other catalog entries

The first two Descartes references in the parent folder match existing DeepMind statements. For the entries below, a maintainer could consider a separate solved variant. Changing the research status of the broad statement would require a proof of the missing case. The Lean declarations here spell out what an external proof reference could support.

| Upstream question | What the packet establishes | What remains |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Descartes uniqueness](https://github.com/google-deepmind/formal-conjectures/blob/ea8e3e198ded6fc73efb7f5653b74b28ac4ae1a8/FormalConjectures/Wikipedia/DescartesNumber.lean#L166-L170) | `second_descartes_restrictions` gives $m>10^{12}$ and at least five primes in $m$. Also, a prime cube divides $mw$ or at least seven primes divide $mw$. | Whether another Descartes number exists. |
| [Almost perfect numbers](https://github.com/google-deepmind/formal-conjectures/blob/ea8e3e198ded6fc73efb7f5653b74b28ac4ae1a8/FormalConjectures/Wikipedia/AlmostPerfectNumbers.lean#L48-L50) | `odd_almostPerfect_witness` gives $n>10^{12}$ and at least five distinct prime factors for every odd solution $n>1$ of $1+σ(n)=2n$. | Larger odd candidates and even non-powers of two. |
| [OEIS A388012](https://oeis.org/A388012) | `five_thirds_residue_primes` forces two distinct prime divisors congruent to 1 modulo 5 in every positive solution of $3σ(n)=5n$. | Existence at equality. The pinned DeepMind tree has no entry numbered 388012. |
| [Odd perfect conjecture and Ochem–Rao](https://github.com/google-deepmind/formal-conjectures/blob/ea8e3e198ded6fc73efb7f5653b74b28ac4ae1a8/FormalConjectures/Wikipedia/PerfectNumbers.lean#L83-L96) | `ochem_rao_of_sixth_power_free` gives both $n>10^{1500}$ and at least 101 prime factors counted with multiplicity when no prime sixth power divides $n$. | The sixth-power branch for the full published bound. The existence question also remains open. |
| [Quasiperfect numbers](https://github.com/google-deepmind/formal-conjectures/blob/ea8e3e198ded6fc73efb7f5653b74b28ac4ae1a8/FormalConjectures/Wikipedia/QuasiperfectNumbers.lean#L46-L48) | `quasiperfect_witness` gives an odd square with at least four distinct prime factors, and at least seven when $3∤n$. | The entry asks for an actual witness to $σ(n)=2n+1$. |
| [Ore’s harmonic numbers, A001599](https://github.com/google-deepmind/formal-conjectures/blob/ea8e3e198ded6fc73efb7f5653b74b28ac4ae1a8/FormalConjectures/OEIS/1599.lean#L89-L90) | `ore_no_prime_power` rules out every positive power of a prime. `ore_odd_of_at_most_two_primes` uses the project theorem `odd_oreHarmonic_three_le_primeFactors` to rule out a nontrivial odd witness with at most two distinct prime factors. | Odd candidates with three or more distinct primes. |
